[[The Mechanism of Mind (Edward De Bono) (z-library.sk, 1lib.sk, z-lib.sk).pdf|The Mechanism of Mind]]: An Annotated Bibliography
- The author provides a brief overview on how the mind functions. The author provides different iterations following each function.
- De Bono, Edward. (January 1969). The Mechanism of Mind. Jonathan Cape. (p. 187)
	- "A gradual moulding of the contours can only take place if what has been moulded retains its form until the next stage in the moulding process" (De Bono 187). This examines how patterns functions: take a look at how  it must retain its form, how something gradually goes into the next stage, and the requirements within the process. A few questions I have include the following: What exactly is the whole process? How can this relate to patterns specifically?
	- "In functional terms the only important point about these internal patterns is that they dominate the memory-surface. Whenever an internal pattern is activated then the active area on the memory-surface tends to include that pattern" (De Bono 192).
