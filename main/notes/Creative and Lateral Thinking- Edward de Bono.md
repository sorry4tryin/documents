	- Parallel thinking: to think in associations (lesser associations compared to lateral thinking or schizophrenia; design without judgment—de Bono's term). Burgh explains that parallel thinking rejects "the adversarial framework", identifying it to be introduced through "claims in parallel instead of in opposition to each other" (Burgh 186). Moreover, parallel thinking is used for the purpose, of what de Bono believes, "emphasizes cooperative and coordinated thinking" (Burgh 186) This raises questions included in the following: is parallel thinking more effective than lateral thinking;
