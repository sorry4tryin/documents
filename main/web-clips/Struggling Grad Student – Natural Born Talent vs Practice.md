## notes
- it takes practice to become better, because talent alone can only propel an individual so far 
	- if you are afraid to take a test, it shows that you are not prepared for the topic evaluation because that is all a test is.
	- "practice makes perfect" which commonly implies that whenever talent runs out in terms of utility, the talent makes a imperfect outcome. though when you combine practice with that imperfect outcome of talent then you create an outcome that is perfect.
- become independent when it comes to studying
	- don't rely on the teacher to explain everything to you about the topic. at least take the time out of your day to sit and conform the topic's language to your own understanding to observe what the topic is about.
		- practice from there; consistency is key from there
