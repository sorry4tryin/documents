## notes
- to write an essay, means "to attempt" to interpret a piece of literature through words; referenced from *Michel de Montaigne*, where he uses essays as "attempts" of interpretations of his thoughts into writing. 
	- *Michel de Montaigne*: a significant writer during the french renaissance
	- considered to be a focused subject: the discussion is restricted to this one subject to reduce confusion and improve precision
	- "long, systematic discourse": the discussion, similar above, is restricted to expand further with a mass amount of structure to improve precision
