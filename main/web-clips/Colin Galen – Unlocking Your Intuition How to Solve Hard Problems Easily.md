## notes
- intuition is the fast, most effective function of the brain
	- mainly used when solving problems
- brain has two sides:
	- slow brain (takes conscious molding/training):
		- effort
		- processing
		- reasoning
		- conscious thinking
		- insight
			- similar to intuition, though insight is the ability to create new solutions off of new knowledge.
	- fast brain (completely unconscious): 
		- intuition
			- functions off of past knowledge. this knowledge includes the knowledge of the problem (what occurs in the situation, the current aspects, and relations to other concepts).
				- more past knowledge = better intuition
				- though it intuition is dependent on past knowledge, intuition needs to be justified to prevent any logical errors
					- proof techniques
					- proof practice
			- relies on the "slow" side of the brain
			- may be a gut feeling; think about it like a sense
			- recognizable as the first thought 
		- feel-based thinking
		- patterns
		- unconscious thinking
- both parts of the brain should be complementary to each other, instead of independently relying on a certain part to solve all of them.
- create a mental map between two concepts:
	- problem components
	- solution ideas 
		-  don't focus on the solutions itself but also elements of how to come up with the solution in similar or unfamiliar scenarios 
			- ask thoughtful questions 
			- notice applied simplifications
				- common thought processes
			- look for new associations for a certain problem
	-  slow solving:
		- great for understanding how problems and solutions function together
		- long-term wise: improves logic and reasoning; improves intuition relative to the rate of logic
	- fast mapping:
		- improves intuition faster
- memory is important
	- remembering items is effective when you:
		- come up with the solution yourself
		- spend ample time on the problem
		- understand why
- nature vs. nurture
	- nature: outcome due to genetic history
	- nurture: outcome due to environmental circumstances 
		- proves that you have control over your life if you start in the present moment that will have an effect on yourself in the long-term viewpoint
			- passion is important
