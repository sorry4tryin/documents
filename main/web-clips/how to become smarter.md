the most known way of becoming smarter comes from reading books. suppose reading books is similar to scrolling on social media: if individual A is on social media, therefore not retaining the information given--or understanding it, then individual B reading a book is not getting smarter. this concludes, that reading books will not make an individual smarter. for individual to become smart, they must have the ability to understand and interpret a select topic. 
to be smart, means to have the ability to understand something. when an individual understands something, they have the ability to interpret a select topic based on their own perspective. another similar aspect of understanding shows when an individual can either perform an action based on a select topic or provide instructions on how the action can be performed. 




in order to practice the information, one method starts with writing essays. writing essays allows the individual to convey their thoughts onto paper, allowing them to precisely inform others what they actually understand about a certain topic. therefore, if you are unable to write a essay on the topic you believe you understand, you do not understand the topic at all.
