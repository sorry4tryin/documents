## notes
- as an individual we should be able to understand the production of art: how it was made, the meaning behind it, even the etymology that surrounds that meaning; or whats the purpose? 
	- appearance doesn't matter. if we judge a piece just off of appearance we wont get to the real reason its there. 
		- the ability to produce isn't similar to the ability to understand. 
			- producing a piece of art, physically drawing or painting, isn't similar to understanding a piece of art due to the fact that there is a reason behind the actions of producing. this mainly includes the question "why did they pick that shade of that color?" everything has a question behind it which leads us to comprehend the image or creative spark a person had when producing a piece of art. so in order to have the ability to produce you must understand.
			- mostly evident in the trend death cycle (diagram in the video)
	- the main reason this occurs is because understanding cannot be due to external factor, but from internal factors. a person must understand the works of a creator themselves.
		- because works from a creator is deeper than just conventional decisions, but explicit description that outline the reflection of ourselves.
		- knowledge is not linear, so connections between ideas wont be either.
- our generation has easy accessibility to creative actions that it blurs the actual talent.
- convenience is somewhat like a dual edge sword.
	- though something that takes a short time to create may appear to take longer than usual it still lacks that introspection. this can create deception and unrealistic expectations surrounding the production of art.
- articulation is vague
	- we may look at something that is eye-catching to us, but aren't able to describe the exact feeling with the correct language.
		- make
	- image illiterate
		- being unable to describe ones own thought about a piece (in the context of images and visual fluency)
			- why this works
			- how this make them feel
			- how deliberate a reference is being used the point of our opinion useless
